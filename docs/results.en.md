### How to read

- **Hallucination rate** = extracted items that don't exist in the expected
  output (1 - precision). The closer to 0%, the better.
- **`uncertain_items` usage** = cases where the reference marked uncertainty
  and the model also marked it (should be 100%).
- Fields are compared as normalized sets (accents/case removed); teeth by FDI
  notation.
- Results on 100% synthetic data — no real patient was used.
